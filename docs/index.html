Academic Heritage & Research Philosophy

The "T-Shaped" Geospatial Profile

Computer scientists frequently ignore spatial dependence (Tobler's First Law), while classical geographers lack deep neural network implementation skills. My trajectory bridges both worlds.

1. Regional Planning Domain

BSc in Geography and Regional Planning from UCC. Practical municipal internship at the Physical Planning Department formulating statutory Medium-Term Development Plans (MTDP), cadastral zoning, and resolving customary stool vs. municipal land tenure conflicts.

2. Methodological Rigor & GeoAI

Custom PyTorch 1D/2D CNNs, SegFormer, SAMGeo, and Google Earth Engine. Zero data leakage enforced through Spatial Block Cross-Validation (GroupKFold over 0.01° grids) to eliminate spatial autocorrelation bias.

3. Pedagogy & Community Mapping

Instructed 100+ students as Teaching Assistant, authoring the 13-module Urban Spatial Analytics Handbook. YouthMappers Organizer mobilizing participatory OpenStreetMap field campaigns for coastal flood and infrastructure mapping.

The 4-Tier Project Architecture

Every project in this portfolio adheres strictly to an empirical four-stage progression from territorial challenge to actionable governance:

01

Spatial Problem

Identifying real-world vulnerabilities: unmonitored peri-urban sprawl, fertilizer runoff, permafrost thaw, dam spillage flooding, or transit healthcare deserts.

02

Methodological Pipeline

Ingesting multi-sensor Earth observation feeds (Sentinel-2, Landsat, UAV drone, OSM) into custom PyTorch deep learning models, GEE scripts, or graph networks.

03

Model Validation

Enforcing Spatial Block CV, Grad-CAM XAI interpretability, confusion matrices, and competitive international benchmarking with zero data leakage.

04

Policy & Planning Impact

Offloading predictions into statutory zoning, variable-rate nitrogen prescriptions, emergency flood evacuation routes, and LUSPA infrastructure master plans.
