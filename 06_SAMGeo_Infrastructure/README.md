## 📌 Overview
Applies Meta's **Segment Anything Model for Geospatial Data (SAMGeo)** with Vision Transformer backbones for automated zero-shot and prompt-based structural feature extraction.

### Applications:
- Automatic mask generation and point/box prompt segmentation on sub-meter aerial imagery.
- Rapid mapping of campus infrastructure at the University of Cape Coast (Sam Jonah Library, Science Lecture Theatres, administrative complexes).
